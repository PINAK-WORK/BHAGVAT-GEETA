### ✨ About

Experience the complete Bhagavad Gita without needing an internet connection. This app brings all 18 chapters and verses directly to your device. It features the original shlokas, clear transliterations, and detailed meanings available in Hindi, English, and Gujarati.


<br>

### 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📱 **100% Offline** | Read the entire sacred text locally on your device without needing the internet. |
| 📖 **All 18 Chapters** | Complete access to every single verse of the Bhagavad Gita. |
| 🌍 **Multi-Language** | Get clear translations and deep meanings in Hindi, English, and Gujarati. |
| 🕉️ **Complete Insights** | Includes the original shloka, transliteration, translation, and meaning for every verse. |
